PhotoDissect Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

PhotoDissect is an iPhone app, published by Bigdatum Services, that helps you find and clean up duplicate, similar and unwanted photos, videos, files and contacts. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it keeps, and what it shares.

The short version

  • PhotoDissect does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • All analysis runs on your iPhone. Your photos, videos, files and contacts never leave your device.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

What the app accesses and why

  • Photos. To scan your library for duplicates, look-alikes, screenshots, blurry shots and large items, and to delete, compress, convert, trim or hide the items you choose. You can grant full or limited access, and change it any time in Settings.
  • Files. Only folders you pick yourself, to find duplicate, old and large files. The app keeps a bookmark to each saved folder on your device so it can rescan it later.
  • Contacts. Only when you open the duplicate contacts tool, to find and merge duplicate cards.
  • Notifications. Only if you turn on the monthly reminder, to tell you how much space can be freed.
  • Background processing. If you turn on the overnight scan, iOS may run the app while your iPhone charges to check for new items.

What is stored on your device

To avoid re-analyzing your library each time, the app stores scan results, your settings, saved folder bookmarks, cleanup totals and the data shown in the Home Screen widget. These stay on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

The app also keeps two small entries in the iOS keychain: how many free cleanups you have used, and the most recent date the App Store signed one of your purchases. They let the free allowance and subscription checks work correctly. They contain no personal information, and iOS may keep them after the app is deleted.

Purchases

PhotoDissect Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks Apple-signed purchase records on your device to unlock Pro. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Sharing

We don't share, sell or rent any data, because we don't collect any. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only network traffic comes from Apple's own services, such as the App Store and iCloud Photos.

Deleted and replaced items

Photos and videos you delete go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days. Compressing, trimming or converting an item saves a new copy and moves the original to Recently Deleted. Files you delete are removed from the folder you chose.

Children

PhotoDissect is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
