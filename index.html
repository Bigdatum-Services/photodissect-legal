PhotoDissect Support

PhotoDissect frees up space on your iPhone by finding duplicate and similar photos, large videos, screenshots, old files and duplicate contacts. Everything runs on your device.

Common questions

How do I restore PhotoDissect Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in PhotoDissect and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Pro is also shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PhotoDissect. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

I deleted something by mistake.

Open the Photos app, go to Albums › Recently Deleted, and recover it. Items stay there for 30 days.

Why can't some items be compressed?

Live Photos, slo-mo, time-lapse, Portrait and RAW photos are left untouched so they keep their effects. Items that are already efficiently encoded are skipped because re-encoding wouldn't save space.
